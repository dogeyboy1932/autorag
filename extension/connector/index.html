Autorag bridge

Leave this tab open and your desktop agent can search everything you have kept while browsing. Close it and the bridge goes away. Nothing is uploaded — the relay listens on loopback only.

This page exists because of two measured limits: a ws://127.0.0.1 socket cannot be opened from an https:// page, and WebMCP refuses tool registration on a chrome-extension:// origin. A page served over plain http on localhost is the one place that satisfies both.
